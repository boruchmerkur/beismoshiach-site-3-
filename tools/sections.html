1. 1796

    Tanya is printed

    The Alter Rebbe puts the inner teaching into a book, in order, for anyone who can read.

  2. 1827

    The Tzemach Tzedek

    A leadership that runs either side of the year the Zohar names, and leaves thousands of pages behind it.

  3. 5600

    בשית מאה שנין לשתיתאה יתפתחון תרעי דחכמתא לעילא ומבועי דחכמתא לתתא

    1840

    Zohar I:117a · the gates of wisdom above, and the wellsprings of wisdom below

  4. 1844

    The telegraph

    Morse's line opens between Washington and Baltimore. Information stops travelling at the speed of a horse.

  5. 1847

    Energy is conserved

    Helmholtz states it generally: the quantity is never created and never destroyed, only moved.

  6. 1850

    The second law

    Clausius gives heat a direction, and with it gives the universe an arrow of time.

  7. 1859

    Origin of Species

    Darwin publishes. Whatever else follows from it, the history of life becomes a subject with evidence.

  8. 1861

    Germ theory

    Pasteur shows that decay has a cause you can see under a lens, and medicine changes underneath.

  9. 1865

    Maxwell's field

    Four equations, and light turns out to be a wave in the same field as electricity and magnetism. The first great unification.

  10. 1869

    The periodic table

    Mendeleev arranges the elements and finds gaps where undiscovered ones must sit. The arrangement predicts.

  11. 1897

    Tomchei Temimim

    The Rebbe Rashab founds the yeshiva, and the study of Chassidus is put on an institutional footing.

  12. 1895

    X-rays

    Röntgen photographs the inside of a hand. The opaque turns out to have been transparent all along.

  13. 1900

    The quantum

    Planck finds that energy comes in units. Nothing in physics is continuous again.

  14. 1905

    E = mc²

    Einstein: mass and energy are one quantity in two forms.

  15. 1915

    General relativity

    Gravity is not a force in space but a shape of it.

  16. 1929

    The universe is expanding

    Hubble's measurements give the cosmos a finite past — for most of the history of science, the unpopular position.

  17. 1940

    Chassidus reaches America

    The Frierdiker Rebbe arrives in New York and refuses to treat the new country as a lesser one.

  18. 1951

    The Rebbe accepts leadership

    The dissemination becomes global and deliberate, and the sciences are addressed directly rather than avoided.

  19. 1953

    The structure of DNA

    Watson, Crick and Franklin's data: inheritance is written in a code that can be read.

  20. 1965

    The background glow

    Penzias and Wilson find the light left over from the beginning, still arriving.

  21. 1967

    Electroweak unification

    Two of the four forces turn out to be one. The programme Maxwell started keeps going.

  22. 1990s

    The wellsprings go online

    Chassidus is put on the network, searchable and free, in more languages than it was ever printed in.

  23. 2003

    The human genome

    The sequence is finished. Three billion letters, published for anyone.

  24. 2012

    The Higgs boson

    The last missing piece of the standard model is observed where the theory said it would be.

  25. 2016

    Gravitational waves

    LIGO hears two black holes merge. A prediction from 1915 confirmed by listening.

  26. 2021

    Protein structure predicted

    AlphaFold folds proteins from sequence alone, and a fifty-year problem stops being one.

These are dates set beside dates. The Zohar named a year; this is what the year produced. Read as chronology it is striking, and it is offered as that — not as a demonstration that one caused the other.

בשית מאה שנין לשתיתאה יתפתחון תרעי דחכמתא לעילא ומבועי דחכמתא לתתא

In the six-hundredth year of the sixth millennium the gates of wisdom above will open, and the wellsprings of wisdom below, and the world will be set to enter the seventh millennium.

Zohar I:117a · on Bereishis 7:11, the 600th year of Noach's life
The finding

The year is 5600 — 1840. From that decade the telegraph, thermodynamics, field theory, germ theory and the periodic table arrive in close order, and the rate has not slowed since. The Rebbe reads the two halves together: Chassidus opening above, the sciences below.

Likkutei Sichos vol. 15, p. 42ff · the Sicha of Parshas Noach

Knowledge is described covering the earth the way water covers the sea — total, ordinary, available to everyone rather than to scholars.

Yeshayahu 11:9 · Rambam, Hilchos Melachim 12:5
The finding

Universal access to the world's knowledge — searchable, translated, in every pocket — went from impossible to unremarkable inside one lifetime. The infrastructure the verse assumes now exists.

The Rebbe returned often to a plain point: if there is one Author, the laws should ultimately be one law. Unity in the world is evidence about its origin, not a convenience of physics.

Devarim 6:4 · Emunah u'Mada

Physics has spent a century collapsing separate forces into fewer descriptions and treats a single unified account as the goal rather than a hope. The direction of travel is toward one.

Yechezkel is shown a valley of dry bones reassembling — sinew, then flesh, then breath. The order of operations is stated plainly.

Yechezkel 37:12
The finding

Biology no longer treats the body's decline as strictly one-directional. Cells are reprogrammed to earlier states, tissue is regrown, and reversibility is a research programme rather than a fantasy.

The Rambam's picture of the era is material before it is mystical: enough for everyone, so that attention is freed for something else.

Rambam, Hilchos Melachim 12:5
The finding

Yield, energy density and the cost of making things have moved further in two centuries than in the twenty before them. Scarcity is increasingly a distribution problem rather than a production one.

Time has a first page. Not an eternal universe running backwards forever, but a beginning — which for most of the history of science was the unpopular position.

Bereishis 1:1

Cosmology settled on an expanding universe with a finite past. Worth saying plainly: the models describe, they do not legislate, and the fit here is a resemblance rather than a proof.
